小星球隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 24 日

小星球（英文名 Cherish）是一款只给你自己用的追星收藏 App。原则很简单：你的照片和记录只属于你。我们不收集、看不到，也不上传到我们的服务器。小星球没有账号系统，也没有自己的服务器。

1. 我们不收集任何个人信息

小星球不收集你的姓名、手机号、位置、通讯录、设备标识或使用行为，不接入广告和第三方统计 SDK，不做用户画像。

2. 你的内容存在哪里

  • 你选进来的图片、视频、链接，以及时间线、章节和纪念日，保存在你的手机上。
  • 如果你登录了 iCloud 并打开了 iCloud 云盘，这些文件会通过苹果的 iCloud 放进你自己的 iCloud 账户，占用你自己的空间。这一过程由苹果提供，适用苹果隐私政策。我们无法访问这些文件。
  • 我们只在 iCloud 键值存储里放两个标记：是否已经创建过星球，以及试用从哪一天开始。标记里没有照片，也没有你写的文字，只用来在换手机时接着恢复、并避免试用被反复重置。

3. 我们请求的权限

  • 照片：只读取你主动选中的照片的拍摄日期，用来排进时间线。从这里删掉一条记录时，相册里的原图还在。
  • 通知：只有你打开纪念日提醒之后，才用手机本地通知提醒生日、出道日和入坑周年。通知在手机上生成，不经过任何服务器。

以上权限都可以随时在系统“设置”里关闭。

4. 水印上的日期

识别照片上的日期，使用苹果系统自带的文字识别，在你的手机上完成。图片不会离开你的设备。

5. 购买

七天试用和一次买断都通过 App Store 完成。试用结束不会扣款，也不会自动续费。支付信息由苹果处理，我们拿不到你的卡号，只能通过苹果确认这个 Apple 账号是否已经买断。

6. 删除你的数据

你可以在 App 里删掉任意一条收藏，也可以导出全部图片和视频（标记为仅自存的不会导出）。删除 App 会清掉这台手机上的数据。iCloud 里的副本可在“设置 > [你的名字] > iCloud”中管理。

7. 未成年人

我们不会主动收集任何人的个人信息，包括未成年人。未成年人购买前，请先征得监护人同意。

8. 政策更新

如果本政策有变化，我们会更新本页面并修改生效日期。

9. 联系我们

有问题请发邮件至 eliozhao@live.com。

Cherish Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Cherish (小星球) is a private photo journal for one person and one idol. Your photos and notes stay yours. We do not collect them, we cannot see them, and we do not upload them to a server of ours. There is no account, and we do not run a content server.

1. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, phone number, location, contacts, device identifiers, or how you use the app. There are no ads and no third-party analytics.

2. Where your things live

  • Photos, videos, links, the timeline, chapters, and anniversaries are stored on your phone.
  • If you are signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive on, those files sync through Apple into your iCloud account and use your storage. That is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy. We cannot open those files.
  • iCloud key-value storage holds only two markers: whether a planet has been created, and the day a trial started. They contain no photos and no notes. They let a new phone resume, and they keep the trial from restarting.

3. Permissions

  • Photo Library. We read the capture date of photos you choose, so they can sit on the right day. Removing an item inside Cherish leaves the original in your library.
  • Notifications. If you turn anniversary reminders on, the phone schedules them locally. Nothing is sent to a server.

You can turn either permission off in Settings.

4. Dates printed on a photo

Reading a date off a photo uses Apple’s on-device text recognition. The picture does not leave your phone.

5. Purchases

The 7-day trial and the one-time purchase go through the App Store. The trial does not charge you, and it does not renew. Apple processes payment. We never see your card, only whether this Apple Account owns the lifetime unlock.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete any item in the app, and you can export your photos and videos. Items you marked private are left out of export and sharing. Deleting the app removes what is on that phone. Copies in iCloud are managed under Settings > [your name] > iCloud.

7. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. A child should have a parent or guardian’s permission before buying.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date.
